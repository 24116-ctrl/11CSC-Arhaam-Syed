211 million acres have been cut down across the
world. every minute 27,000+ trees are being cut
down at this very moment. Every day the number
adds up to 41-42 Million trees.
The Amazon rainforest is the largest tropical
rainforest. The Amazon rainforest produces 20%
of the oxygen on Earth. Around 1.4 Billion Trees
are cut down in Amazon each year, Resulting in
more carbon dioxide increasing global warming
gasses in the atmosphere.

Deforestation is dangerous that is why we need
to stand up and protest against it.
Carbon levels are rising dangerously high,
which causes temperature to rise.
This is a big issue because higher temperature
means the ice in Antarctica will melt which results
in sea level rise.
